About LoanPredict AI

A production-grade, transparent Machine Learning platform for responsible credit risk assessment.

Problem Statement & Executive Overview

Traditional credit underwriting models operate as opaque "black boxes." When a loan applicant is rejected or categorized as high default risk, regulatory standards (such as the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Article 22 Right to Explanation) in Europe) mandate clear, auditable, and actionable adverse action notices.

LoanPredict AI bridges the gap between state-of-the-art predictive accuracy and full regulatory explainability by coupling an ensemble Random Forest classifier with SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations).

System Architecture

Modular Full-Stack Pipeline
  • Frontend: Responsive UI with dynamic multi-step wizard, Chart.js visualizations, and CSS design system.
  • Backend: Python Flask REST API orchestrating ML inference and XAI explanations.
  • Model Engine: Scikit-Learn Random Forest (200 estimators, max depth 15) with StandardScaler normalization.
  • Explainability Layer: Fast TreeExplainer for SHAP feature attribution and LimeTabularExplainer for local condition rules.
  • Persistence: Thread-safe SQLite audit store for all historical decisions.

Regulatory & Ethical Compliance

Transparent AI Governance
  • Adverse Action Notice Support: Instantly isolates the top negative contributing features (e.g. debt-to-income, credit score).
  • Bias & Fairness Auditing: Gender and marital status features are monitored to guarantee non-discriminatory credit assessment.
  • Auditability: Every prediction persists with its full mathematical feature contribution vector for retrospective compliance review.
