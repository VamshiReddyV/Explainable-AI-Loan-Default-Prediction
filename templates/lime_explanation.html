Local Surrogate Philosophy

While the global model (200 decision trees) is non-linear and complex, any complex decision boundary can be locally approximated by a simple linear model: g(z') = w0 + ∑ wi z'i.

Rule-Based Discretization

Continuous numerical inputs (such as credit score and income) are discretized into quartile buckets so lending officers receive actionable thresholds (e.g. Credit Score ≤ 600).
