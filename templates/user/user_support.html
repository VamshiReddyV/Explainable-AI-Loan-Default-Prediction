What is SHAP and how does it explain my default prediction?

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) is an advanced framework rooted in cooperative game theory. Instead of treating the AI model as a "black box," SHAP quantifies the exact contribution of each individual feature (such as your Credit Score, Monthly Income, or Loan Term) to moving your predicted default risk above or below the baseline population average (35.0%).

Positive SHAP values indicate factors that increased your risk score, while negative SHAP values represent positive protective factors that helped reduce your risk.

What is LIME and how does it differ from SHAP?

LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations) analyzes your specific application by generating small perturbations (slight variations) around your financial parameters to create an interpretable linear surrogate model.

While SHAP provides global consistency and exact mathematical attribution across all feature interactions, LIME provides intuitive, localized "if-then" decision boundaries showing what conditions directly influenced your individual decision.

How are Default Probability and Risk Tiers calibrated?

Our machine learning ensemble outputs a continuous calibrated probability from 0.0% to 100.0%. The scores are mapped into three standardized banking tiers:

  • Low Risk (0% – 30%): High eligibility. Standard prime underwriting approval with preferential interest rates.
  • Medium Risk (30% – 60%): Moderate risk. Approved subject to income verification, collateral, or co-signer evaluation.
  • High Risk (60% – 100%): Elevated default likelihood. Recommendations are provided to optimize debt burden before formal institutional submission.
How can I quickly lower my predicted default risk?

Based on our SHAP sensitivity analysis across thousands of evaluated loans, the three most impactful adjustments are:

  1. Increase Loan Term: Moving from a 24-month to a 36- or 48-month term lowers monthly EMI obligation, significantly boosting debt-to-income affordability.
  2. Moderate Requested Loan Amount: Keeping the principal within 4–5x your verified monthly salary substantially reduces the model's loss-given-default penalty.
  3. Consolidate Existing Open Loans: Applicants with 0 or 1 active loans receive strong positive SHAP protective credits compared to those with 3+ active loans.
Does running multiple assessments hurt my official credit score?

No. Absolutely not. Assessments conducted on LoanPredict AI are "soft evaluations" executed entirely within our private sandbox inference engine. They are never reported as hard inquiries to credit bureaus and have zero adverse impact on your CIBIL, Experian, or Equifax credit standing.

How do I present my XAI Explanation report to a lending bank?

Navigate to your My Predictions tab, click on any completed assessment, and select Export PDF Report. The exported document contains a certified Explainable AI audit sheet featuring both SHAP factor values and LIME weights, providing empirical evidence of repayment capability to bank credit underwriters.
